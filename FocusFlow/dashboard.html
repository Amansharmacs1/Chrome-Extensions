How FocusFlow Blocking Works
Privacy-First Chrome MV3 Architecture
FocusFlow uses Chrome's official DeclarativeNetRequest dynamic rules engine. When your focus timer runs, rules are added directly into Chrome's network pipeline. When your session pauses or ends, the rules are instantly cleared.
Browser Safeguards & Limitations
In accordance with Google Chrome security standards, extensions cannot block internal browser management pages (such as chrome://settings, chrome://extensions, or the Chrome Web Store). Subdomains of added domains (e.g. m.youtube.com) are automatically protected.
