# 🛡️ WebNotes Privacy Policy & Data Guarantees

> **Effective Date:** October 2026  
> **Extension:** WebNotes — Remember What Matters  
> **Status:** 100% Local, Offline & Private

WebNotes was built with a fundamental commitment: **your notes, research, and browsing habits are private and belong exclusively to you.**

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection

- **No Analytics**: WebNotes does not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Sentry, or any third-party tracking libraries.
- **No Telemetry**: No crash reports or usage statistics are transmitted over the internet.
- **No User Profiling**: We do not create, maintain, or sell user profiles or advertising IDs.
- **No Account Required**: You never need to enter an email address, password, or credit card.

---

## 2. Local-Only Storage

All notes, quotations, webpage titles, URLs, tags, and settings created in WebNotes are saved locally on your device in Chrome's sandboxed storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

- Your data is never transmitted to any remote server or cloud database.
- If you uninstall the extension without exporting a backup, Chrome permanently removes your local extension storage.
- You have complete control over your data: you can export it to a JSON file at any time using the built-in backup tool, or erase it completely via the Settings screen.

---

## 3. Webpage Access & Restricted Pages

WebNotes only accesses the URL, title, and selected text of the **active tab** at the exact moment you:
1. Click the WebNotes extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.
2. Select text and click "Save to WebNotes" in the right-click menu.
3. Click the "Capture Selection" button inside the popup.

WebNotes never runs in the background across your other tabs, and it automatically rejects and protects restricted URLs (e.g., `chrome://`, `chrome-extension://`, `about:blank`, Chrome Web Store).

---

## 4. Contact & Auditing

WebNotes is completely open source. You can inspect every line of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in this repository to verify these privacy assurances yourself.
